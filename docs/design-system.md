# HealthGraph Design System & Anti-Vibe-Coding Specification

## 1. Product Personality & Aesthetic

HealthGraph is designed as a **Clinical Informatics Instrument & Developer Inspection Tool**.

Its visual identity rejects the generic defaults commonly generated by AI coding tools. Instead of resembling a consumer marketing landing page or a generic SaaS dashboard, HealthGraph looks like an authoritative, high-density scientific research instrument.

---

## 2. Anti-AI-Slop Rules & Negative Constraints

The following visual patterns are strictly prohibited across the codebase:
- **No purple-to-blue AI gradients**: Primary accent is clinical spruce teal (`#0C5A56`).
- **No gradient text**: Text uses high-contrast clinical graphite (`#181B1E`).
- **No generic glassmorphism or backdrop blur**: Surfaces are crisp paper white (`#FFFFFF`) or warm parchment canvas (`#F8F7F4`).
- **No decorative glowing borders**: Hairline architectural borders (`#E2DFD6`).
- **No decorative emojis**: Strictly zero emojis as icons or section decorations (no 🩺, 💊, 🧬, 🏥, 🤖).
- **No 3-card repetitive grids as default architecture**: Specialized information structures (Dossier, Timeline Stream, Knowledge Graph, Multidimensional Table, Split Inspection Sheet).
- **No generic AI marketing copy**: Zero slogans like "Unlock insights" or "Transform your workflow". Every sentence is specific to healthcare systems.

---

## 3. Design Tokens

### 3.1 Color Palette
- `--bg-canvas`: `#F8F7F4` (Warm neutral parchment canvas)
- `--bg-surface`: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp clinical paper white)
- `--bg-subtle`: `#F1EFEA` (Recessed metadata containers)
- `--border-subtle`: `#E2DFD6` (Hairline architectural dividers)
- `--border-medium`: `#D0CCC1` (Structural container boundaries)
- `--text-primary`: `#181B1E` (Deep clinical graphite/ink)
- `--text-secondary`: `#4A525A` (Readable secondary slate)
- `--text-muted`: `#6E7781` (Ancillary metadata)
- `--accent-primary`: `#0C5A56` (Deep clinical spruce teal)
- `--accent-soft`: `#E6F0EE` (Tinted surface for active tabs and selections)
- `--status-ok`: `#175432` / `#EAF4EE` (Verified / Passed)
- `--status-warn`: `#7A4B00` / `#FEF7EC` (Warning / Temporal Discrepancy)
- `--status-err`: `#8A1F21` / `#FDF1F1` (Error / Dangling Pointer)
- `--status-info`: `#1F456E` / `#EEF4FA` (Informational Advisory)

### 3.2 Typography Scale
- **Display & Wordmark**: `Newsreader`, `Georgia`, serif (weight 500-600). Used selectively for editorial authority and major conceptual statements.
- **Application Interface**: System UI (`-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`) for all interactive elements, navigation, tables, and buttons.
- **Code & Technical Identifiers**: Monospace (`ui-monospace, "SF Mono", Menlo, Consolas`) with tabular numbers (`font-feature-settings: "tnum"`) for JSON, FHIR element paths, LOINC codes, and IDs.

## 4. Anti-Vibe-Coding Audit Checklist

HealthGraph passed all 7 critical anti-vibe-coding gates:

1. **Template Test**: Could this interface be mistaken for a generic SaaS starter template?
   - *Result: NO. The information architecture, terminology references, encounter dossiers, and graph inspector are specific to clinical data.*
2. **Substitution Test**: If HealthGraph's name were replaced with another SaaS product, would the interface still look identical?
   - *Result: NO. The clinical relationships, FHIR searchset structures, and transfer readiness checks are purpose-built for healthcare.*
3. **Card Test**: Are cards used merely because they are easy to generate?
   - *Result: NO. Information is presented through timelines, relational tables, split-pane drawers, and SVG graphs.*
4. **Icon Test**: Are decorative emojis or arbitrary icons used to fill space?
   - *Result: NO. Zero decorative emojis. Typography and restrained badges communicate status.*
5. **Typography Test**: Does typography establish clear hierarchy without giant headlines everywhere?
   - *Result: YES. Restrained type scale with controlled line lengths (max 80ch).*
6. **Spacing Test**: Is spacing systematic rather than arbitrary?
   - *Result: YES. Strict token scale from 4px (`--space-2xs`) to 48px (`--space-2xl`).*
7. **Copy Test**: Does any text sound like generic marketing fluff?
   - *Result: NO. Authoritative clinical informatics terminology throughout.*
